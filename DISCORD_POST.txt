vamm capstone q2 2026

volatility adaptive amm on solana

what it does

watches its own trades to measure how crazy the market is right now
when things are chill it keeps the curve flat and fees cheap
when things get wild it tightens the curve and raises fees to protect LPs
all on chain no oracles no admin no one pulling levers

how

every swap drops a price breadcrumb
tick differences give us log returns without any float math
an ewma tracks variance smoothed so one whale trade doesnt fake a spike
sigma feeds two things A and the fee
A ramps over an hour fees slide at 10 bps per slot max
both moves are too slow to arb too fast to ignore

six instructions

init pool sets up all the accounts and pdas
swap does the trade with a dynamic fee
add liquidity gives you d based lp shares
remove liquidity burns your lp and gives you your tokens back
update vol recalculates A and fee from the ewma anyone can call it
update curve interpolates the A ramp also permissionless

no admin key the pool authority is a pda only the program signs for it
all accounts are pdas seeded from pool state so you can find them without an indexer
ring buffers cross check the ewma so manipulation gets caught

built with anchor on solana devnet
